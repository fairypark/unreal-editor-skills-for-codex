# Visual-First Production Legacy

Inherit quality gates and cause-level lessons, not scene coordinates, instance
counts, asset paths, light values, or PCG parameters.

## Start from visible intent

1. Inspect available assets before selecting a concept that lets the strongest
   asset families support one coherent environment.
2. Produce two to four concept views covering arrival, hero, route, and reverse
   directions. Select one primary quality reference and record prohibited
   failure imagery.
3. Create a Visual Recipe and Persistent Camera Actors before production
   layout. Any view used for a decision must remain available for recapture.
4. Prove a representative 10-30 m golden slice before broad dressing.
5. Let a separate read-only visual supervisor control stage transitions. Use
   `gpt-5.6-sol`, `xhigh`, and `visual_critic` when supported. Every applicable
   category must score at least 4/5 and any hard failure forces `NO-GO`.

## Keep visual quality authoritative

- During concept matching and golden-slice work, visual quality outranks Actor
  count, instance count, collision completeness, PIE success, performance, and
  technical neatness.
- Do not impose a numerical instance limit. Reject visible tiling, equal
  spacing, repeated silhouettes, and uniform scatter in fixed cameras.
- Default decorative foliage, water plants, small rocks, gravel, debris, and
  story props to `NoCollision`, no overlap events, and no Navigation influence.
- Select Landscape, mesh terrain, or a hybrid from the approved concept and
  golden-slice result. Landscape is common, not a mandatory quality target.
- Add narrow proxy collision after visual approval without moving approved
  placement, then recapture the same cameras.

## Apply proven image-level rules

- Treat empty terrain and uniformly sparse scatter as visible failures. Build
  ecology from overlapping canopy, understory, edge vegetation, ground cover,
  contact debris, density gradients, and intentional gaps.
- Build water plants as colonies with dense local overlap, irregular lobes,
  shoreline contact, and authored open-water corridors. Reject grids, uniform
  rings, repeated flower markers, and exposed repeated bases.
- Allow a darker and softer result when it improves concept fidelity while
  retaining focal readability.
- Use Exponential Height Fog for distance separation and overlapping Local Fog
  Volumes for waterline mist. Evaluate from player height and above. Atmosphere
  cannot replace missing midground silhouettes.
- Make a route read as one width-bearing surface, not a centerline chain,
  cement-like strip, or overlap of independent generators.
- For gravel, use dense small rounded pieces, restrained larger accents,
  embedded contact, believable material variation, and irregular edges.
- Prefer one authored spline or closed route boundary projected to trusted
  terrain height and normal. Merge responsibilities before one final spawn.
- A landmark passes only when an uninformed reviewer can identify it in the
  intended cameras through silhouette, value separation, and believable
  contact.

## Preserve the repair loop

1. Capture clean fixed cameras with stable FOV, exposure, aspect ratio,
   scalability, and warmed temporal history.
2. Give concepts and evidence to the read-only supervisor without the
   builder's intended verdict or persuasive explanation.
3. If the gate fails, freeze cameras and accepted systems. Repair the weakest
   visible system and recapture the same views.
4. Change one major variable per failed iteration. Do not average away a score
   below 4 or advance because the level saved successfully.
5. After three consecutive failures of one macro composition, require a
   documented concept or composition reset.

## Accelerate only approved quality

- Reuse independently approved modules by role, but treat every reused module
  as a candidate in the new biome, scale, terrain, lighting, and camera context.
- Automate setup, deterministic seeds, asset sets, persistent-camera capture,
  evidence receipts, and supervisor packet creation. Keep the aesthetic verdict
  outside builder automation.
- Measure time to a valid concept package, first golden-slice capture,
  independent `GO`, `NO-GO` loop count, and fixed-camera recapture count.
- Never gain speed by skipping concepts, reducing camera coverage, lowering the
  4/5 floor, removing independent review, or scaling a failed slice.
