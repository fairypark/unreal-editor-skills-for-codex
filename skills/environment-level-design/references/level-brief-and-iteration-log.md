## Promote a lesson into the reusable skill only when

- it has appeared in more than one environment or is strongly generalizable;
- it explains a cause and prevention rule, not just an anecdote;
- it contains no project path, map name, Actor name, exact coordinate, private asset name, or user-specific infrastructure;
- it remains useful even if Unreal tool names or schemas change;
- it is concise enough to earn permanent context.
